Meetings from Granola

Meetings you record in Granola come into the vault by themselves, as files in their month, Meetings/2026/10/, the same as Gemini's. DFAI asks Granola for them with an API key; nothing else is needed.

Set it up

  1. Make a key in Granola. In the Granola app: Settings → Connectors → API keys → Create new key, with Personal notes (and Public notes if you want meetings others share with you). It needs a Business or Enterprise plan. Granola shows the key once; copy it.
  2. Paste it into DFAI. Settings (⌘,) → Granola. Paste the key into Granola API key and press Save. DFAI keeps it in this Mac's Keychain, never in the vault, and asks Granola about it once: Settings says Key accepted, or Granola refused this key.
  3. Check Your emails in Settings → Profile. In a meeting you own, Granola calls you “me”. DFAI writes that as You when the meeting's owner is one of those addresses, and as the owner's name otherwise (so a meeting someone shared with you isn't put in your mouth).
  4. That's it. Once the key is accepted, DFAI pulls your meetings straight away. The first pull looks at every meeting Granola has and brings in the ones the vault doesn't already have.

Each Mac that should pull needs the key pasted on it. Meetings come in on whichever Mac is in front of you; the import, linking and corrections then run on the Mac that runs your automations, as for any meeting.

When it pulls

  • As the vault opens — but only after DFAI has synced the vault and caught up with what that sync brought in. That way a meeting another Mac already imported is found where it is now, and isn't written a second time. Each later sync that brings files in does the same: a pull, timed or Sync now, waits until DFAI has caught up with them — and with any sync that lands while it waits — but gives up after two minutes on any one of them. A vault that doesn't sync doesn't wait. Until then Settings says DFAI pulls from Granola once this vault has synced.
  • Every 15 minutes while the vault is open. To turn this off, add granola_periodic: false to the vault's settings note (.atlas/settings.md).
  • When you press Sync now in Settings → Granola. Sync now always looks at every meeting Granola has, so it finds one the timed pulls missed.

Settings shows Pulling from Granola… while it works, then what the last pull did and when — for example 3 new, 12 already in DFAI, 1 refused at 9:40 AM. Details lists each meeting that wasn't written and why, with Open in Granola to see it there. A pull is also a line in Activity, under Meetings — except a timed pull that found nothing new and nothing wrong, which would only be noise.

A meeting DFAI already has is never fetched or changed again — even if you edit it in Granola later. A meeting deleted in Granola stays in DFAI.

When something goes wrong

You seeWhat it means
No key on this Mac, and Sync now greyed outPaste a key, as above.
Granola refused this key, and a notice onceThe key has expired or was deleted. Make a new one in Granola and paste it. DFAI stops pulling on its own until it has a key Granola accepts; Sync now still tries.
A notice after Sync now, such as Granola could not be reachedCheck the connection and press Sync now again. A pull that fails by itself (on open or on the timer) says so in Settings and Activity only, and the next pull tries again.
1 refused or 1 skippedOpen Details. DFAI couldn't read that meeting, or Granola wouldn't give it. Sync now tries it again.
Your emails in settings could not be read or The group addresses in settings could not be readThe settings note holds something under selfEmails (or granola_self_emails) or granola_group_addresses that isn't all addresses — usually a hand edit. Until it's fixed DFAI reads it as no addresses at all, pulls still run, and the card names what it couldn't read. Fix your addresses in Your emails, or fix the note.
Waiting for the index timed out in ActivityA pull waited two minutes for DFAI to catch up with what a sync brought in, and gave up without writing anything. The next sync, or opening the vault again, tries again.

To stop pulling on this Mac, press Forget key…, then Forget key. The meetings already in the vault stay.

Meeting times are written in the vault's time zone for Granola, granola_time_zone in the settings note (Los Angeles when it says nothing), and list addresses that don't look like one can be named in granola_group_addresses — so every Mac writes a meeting the same way.
