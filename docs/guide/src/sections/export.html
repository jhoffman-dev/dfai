Exporting notes

Take a note — or a meeting and its transcript — out of DFAI as Markdown, a PDF or a Google Doc. Exporting only reads; it never changes the note.

Start one

  • A note's … menu → Export…, or the same in the peek's … menu.
  • ⌘K → Export…, for the note on screen — or the peeked one.
  • Several at once: in a view or table, press Select, tick the notes, then Export N notes… on the bar — see Act on several rows.

Pick a format

FormatWhat you get
Markdown, as isThe file exactly as it is, byte for byte. Its pictures are copied to a <name> attachments folder beside it.
Markdown, standaloneReads in any tool. A [[link]] becomes its plain words — or, when the notes it names are exported with it, a relative link to their files. Embedded notes and blocks are written in as quotes, under From <note>. Pictures are copied; callouts stay; Obsidian block ids like ^t0001 are dropped. Tick Keep the properties as frontmatter to keep them at the top.
PDFThe note as it reads in DFAI: title, a summary of its properties, the text, tables, checklists and pictures, in the light theme on US Letter pages. A table row or a line is never cut across two pages. A meeting's transcript keeps each speaker's turn with its time.
Google DocA real Google Doc in your Drive, named as the note, in a folder called DFAI exports.

Choose where

  1. Press Export…. For a file, the Mac's own save dialog asks where it goes.
  2. For several notes, pick a folder: DFAI makes a new dated folder in it, Exported notes 2026-10-21, with the notes side by side and their pictures shared in attachments/.
  3. Cancel the save dialog and nothing is written; the export dialog stays open to try again.

Afterwards a notice says where it went — for one Google Doc, with Open in Google Docs. Activity records it under Exports.

Google Docs

DFAI uses the Google Calendar sign-in from Settings → Google Calendar. The first time, your browser asks once to allow one more permission: making and changing only the files DFAI creates (drive.file) — never anything else in your Drive. The sign-in stays in your Mac's Keychain. If Google isn't connected yet, DFAI says to connect in Settings → Google Calendar first.

When it can't

  • The reason is said: the folder can't be written to, Google isn't connected or wasn't allowed, you're offline, or it's too large — a PDF page over 64 MB, or a Doc over 45 MB with its pictures.
  • Pictures that can't be found, or are too big, are left out, and the notice counts them.
  • A PDF or a Google Doc fetches nothing from the web: a picture on the web becomes its words, linked to its address, and is counted as left out too.
  • An export never goes inside the vault, and never into a folder that's already there: if Kickoff attachments exists beside the file, the pictures go to Kickoff attachments 2. If an export fails part-way, what it made is taken away again; nothing that was there before is touched.
  • If one of several Google Docs fails, the ones already made are listed in Activity, and the notice opens the first. Google being busy for a moment is tried again a few times first.

Claude and scripts can read a note's Markdown through the Local API and MCP; PDFs and Google Docs are made only in the app.
