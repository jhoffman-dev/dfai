Morning brief

A pre-read for each of today's meetings, waiting in the Inbox when you sit down: who you are meeting, where things stand with them, and what to bring up. Claude writes it from what DFAI already knows about the people and companies in the meeting.

Turn it on

  1. Have today's meetings arriving from Google Calendar, linked to the people in them.
  2. Open Automations, press Morning brief, look it over and press Create automation. It is off until you do.
  3. It runs every day at 07:00 on the Mac that runs the vault's automations. Press Run now to brief today's meetings straight away.
  4. "Today" and 07:00 are your main Google calendar's, not the Mac's. On a trip it briefs your calendar's day at 07:00 in your calendar's time zone, once, wherever the Mac is. The Automations page shows its next run and its log on that clock too, and says so when it isn't the Mac's — 07:00 Chicago time. Until DFAI has read your meetings once, it uses the Mac's clock; when a vault opens, the brief waits until DFAI has read the vault's settings.

What you get

  • One note per meeting in Inbox/Briefs/<day>/, named by its start and title: Inbox/Briefs/2026-10-26/09.30 Larkspur Payroll sync.md. It waits in the Inbox like anything else you have not filed.
  • Only meetings with a linked person or company get one. A meeting you declined, one that was cancelled, an all-day event and one with nobody linked get none. On a day with none of those, nothing is written.
  • Claude is handed the meeting and, for each person and company in it, what their brief says: open tasks, who you are waiting on, follow-ups, the last meeting and what came of it, decisions and recent mentions. Every line of the note links the note it is about; a line that links none is left out, and the run's log says which.
  • At most ten meetings are briefed in one run. Run now briefs the rest.

Once a meeting, never over a note

A meeting gets one brief a day, however often the rule runs. The brief records the day and the Google event it is for (atlas_period, atlas_period_item), so your other Mac knows it is made once sync brings it over. Running again the same day makes nothing new and doesn't ask Claude. Filing a brief out of the Inbox keeps it made, wherever you put it. If you delete a brief, the 07:00 run won't bring it back; Run now writes it again. A brief never replaces a note already there: if the name is taken, it gets the next free one, so two meetings with the same time and title get 09.30 Standup.md and 09.30 Standup 2.md.

DFAI sets a brief's properties itself and ignores any Claude suggests, so a brief is never mistaken for one of the day's meetings. Retyping an event note yourself is fine: it stays the meeting's note.

Asleep at 07:00

If the Mac is asleep or DFAI is closed at 07:00, the brief is written when it next wakes that day. It never briefs yesterday's meetings: wake up the next morning before 07:00 and it waits for 07:00.

If one meeting fails

If Claude fails on a meeting, or its brief can't be read together or saved (a full disk, say), the other meetings still get their brief. Activity says once which meeting has none and why, and nothing half-written is left. Run now tries that meeting again. Only opening another vault stops the run part-way; what it made by then is in Activity.

The prompt is .atlas/prompts/morning-brief.md, yours to edit. Today and 07:00 are on your Mac's clock, the same day Today shows.

Briefs are notes, so the Local API and MCP read them as any other note.
