The Inbox: what Claude proposes

Claude never edits a note on its own. What it — or an automation — suggests lands as a proposal, a note waiting in Inbox/Proposals, and nothing it proposes is written until you accept it.

Open the proposals

  1. Open the Inbox: proposals are its Proposals section, above the notes waiting to be filed. The section shows while something waits for an answer, and the Inbox's count includes them.
  2. Each card says what it would make — TASK · Send Mara the payroll file — with why it was proposed and what it would write under it, how sure its maker was (a High, Medium or Low pill; a Low row has a coloured edge, so you check it first), and the line it came from: click [[2026-10-01 Standup#^t0003]] and the meeting opens at that line. A proposal that cites nothing says Cites no source, so you know to check it yourself.
  3. Answer it: Accept, Edit or Reject — each in its own colour on the row, so they are told apart at a glance.

Pick one, filter, accept the sure ones

  • Proposals that disagree are one question. Two proposals conflict when they are of the same kind, from the same line (or the same meeting), for the same slot — the owner of one meeting, say — but say different things: three links for one “Mara Quill”, or two projects for one meeting. They show as one row, Which link for owner of 2026-10-16 Platform Standup?, with a radio per answer. Accept choice accepts the one you picked and, only once that is written, rejects the rest; if the accept is refused nothing is rejected and the question stays. One Undo takes the accept back and opens the others again. Reject all rejects every one. Links to the same note count as one answer however they are written ([[Mara Quill]], [[mara quill|Mara]]), and the same answer proposed twice shows as one card: accepting it rejects the copy, and Accept all high confidence takes it once. A relation that holds many notes (a meeting's people) is never a question: each proposal for it is its own card. Different slots never conflict, and nothing about a question is saved: it is worked out from what is open each time.
  • Filters. All, High confidence (the sure ones that need no choice) and Needs a choice (the questions) narrow the list; the count beside the heading stays the whole.
  • Accept all high confidence accepts every high-confidence proposal that needs no choice, as one run, with one Undo; it shows only while there is one.

What each answer does

  • Accept writes what the card shows and files the proposal in the Archive with state: accepted and answered_via: app — api when Claude answered it for you through the Local API, so the Archive keeps who said yes. The card leaves at once and the next one can be answered straight away; what it makes is written behind it. Answered from the keyboard, the focus moves to the next card, so Enter on the next Accept is all it takes. Closing the window waits for answers still being written. The box at the bottom right says “Accepted: Send Mara the payroll file.” with Open Send Mara the payroll file to go to it, and Undo. If the write fails, the card comes back with why on it, still waiting for an answer.
  • Edit opens what it would write — the title, each property, the body (for a link, just the link) — so you can change it, then Accept as edited. A list shows as items with commas between; an item holding a comma of its own is in double quotes ("[[Larkspur Payroll, Inc.]]", [[Fenn & Co]]). A property you don't touch is written exactly as proposed. Cancel leaves it as it was. The proposal is archived with your edit, so the Archive shows what was actually accepted.
  • Reject writes nothing it proposed and files it in the Archive with state: rejected — kept, never deleted.
  • Several at once. With two or more waiting, each card has a box: tick the ones you want — Shift-click a second box to take every card between — or Select all. The bar says 3 selected with Accept 3 proposals, Reject 3 proposals and Clear. Each is answered in turn exactly as its own Accept or Reject would; one that can't be stays on the list, still ticked, with why on its card — and any card you tick while they are written stays ticked too — and the box says so — “Accepted 2 proposals. 1 was not: its card says why.” One Undo takes back every accept in the batch.
  • Undo after an accept takes away what it made — if nothing has changed it since — and puts the proposal back, open again. The box goes by itself after 5 seconds (it waits while the pointer is on it or the focus is in it), and once it has gone so has the undo; a newer answer's box takes its place. Each box's Undo takes back the accept it names: an undo still being written never takes away a later accept's Undo. A reject is said too, with no Undo. Something that failed with nothing to undo — an undo that could not be done — is said at the top of the proposals instead, and stays until you press ×.

What a proposal can make

Task, Follow-upA new task, with source linking the line it came from and meeting linking the meeting the proposal names — whatever made it from a meeting says which. A follow-up is a task to chase someone.
DecisionA new note of the built-in Decision type — title, date, meeting, project, people — citing its line the same way.
Person, Term, ProjectA new note of that type. A person proposed from a meeting is added to that meeting's People too.
Dashboard, ViewA new dashboard in .atlas/dashboards, or a saved view in .atlas/views — what asking Claude for one proposes. Only Accept in DFAI makes it, never a connected tool. Its card lists the widgets, or the view's type and layout; Edit changes only its name.
LinkAdds one link to a note's relation — Mara Quill's Companies gets [[Larkspur Payroll]]. A link from meeting linking goes in even if the meeting has changed since, but never replaces a link already there: a meeting whose project you set is left with yours.

When it says no

Accept never writes over anything. If it can't go ahead, the reason shows on the card and the proposal stays waiting:

  • “There is already a note at Send Mara the payroll file.md, which may be this one. Edit the title to make another.”
  • “Mara Quill changed since this was proposed, so it was left as it is.” — the note a link would change has been edited since; check it and answer again.
  • “2026-10-06 Payroll sync already links [[Payrun]] in project; this would replace it.” — the meeting got a project since the proposal was made.
  • A proposal, or the note it would change, with unsaved typing: save it first.
  • A proposal may only write where your notes live — apart from a dashboard or view, which goes in its own folder of .atlas — never in .atlas, a hidden folder, a folder DFAI doesn't show (node_modules), the Archive or Inbox/Proposals itself. A title too long for a file name is cut to fit.

Where they live

  • A proposal is an ordinary note of the built-in Proposal type, so it syncs to your other Mac, survives a restart, and shows in queries. Only open ones stay in Inbox/Proposals; one answered that the Archive couldn't take is listed under Answered, still in the Inbox, to archive from its page.
  • Proposal and Decision are built-in types, and every task gains a Meeting link.
  • Claude can list and answer them for you through atlas_proposals — only when you ask it to.
Proposals that arrive by themselves. An automation can ask Claude about each new meeting, or any note, and what it answers lands here as proposals. A ready-made rule for a meeting's action items is coming later in phase 29.
