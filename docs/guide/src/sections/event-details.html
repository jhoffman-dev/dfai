Warning and error details

A warning or an error is one short line wherever it shows. Click its words to read the whole of it.

Where it opens

  • A Warning or Error line on the Activity page. An Info line doesn't open: its one line is all there is.
  • A warning or an error in the box at the bottom right. The box stays while you read its details. Once you close them it goes after a few seconds, as it would have.
  • A red notice at the top of the window, and the notice about edits kept after a failed save.

What it shows

  • The level, Warning or Error, and when it happened.
  • The whole message, wrapped rather than cut to one line. A box or a notice opens to exactly the words it says, however long.
  • About: the note, rule or source it concerns, with Open — the same place the line's own Open goes.
  • Detail: anything more the event kept. A rule that could not run keeps its problem here, past where the line cuts it short; on the web, an index the browser won't store keeps what the browser said.
  • Notes: the other notes the line is about, such as the meetings a rule's run heard.
  • Copy puts all of it on the clipboard as plain text, a fact to a line. The button says Copied for a couple of seconds once it has, and Could not copy. shows beside it if the browser has no clipboard to give.

Keys

  • Tab reaches a line that opens; Enter or Space opens it.
  • Esc, Close or a click outside closes it, and the focus goes back to the line.

It stays until you close it

  • The details stay open, on the words you opened, when the line they came from goes: a box pushed out by newer ones, a notice that cleared itself, an Activity line gathered under its meeting or trimmed from the log.

An Activity line's details hold what the line holds, under the same rules: no note contents, no secrets, no folder paths, no chat note's name. A box or a notice opens to its own words as it showed them.
