Thumbnails

A picture of a note's page, made by DFAI, that fronts its card in a gallery. Any type can have one.

  1. Add a Thumbnail property: to a whole type in Edit type, or to one note with + Add a property → kind Thumbnail.
  2. That's it. A little after you stop typing (about four seconds, once the note is saved), DFAI takes a picture of the page — title, text and pictures — and redoes it whenever the note changes.
  3. Galleries of that type show the picture on each card.

The Thumbnail row on the note shows the picture, with:

  • Generate / Regenerate — take the picture again now. If the note points at a picture on the web, DFAI asks first, because that one can't be brought back.
  • Choose image… — use a picture of your own instead. It's copied into the vault like a pasted image, and stays until you change it.
  • Clear — no thumbnail. It stays cleared (the row says None) until you press Generate; the gallery falls back to the note's cover or first image.

Pictures DFAI makes are kept in .atlas-cache/thumbnails, not in your notes; the note just says auto. A picture you choose is saved in the vault. Board and list cards don't show thumbnails yet.
